### 📑 Executive Summary

This module establishes the foundational context for the entire "Agentic Path" series. It is designed to align technical and non-technical stakeholders on the shift from **Deterministic Software** (Logic) to **Probabilistic Systems** (Generative Agents).

**Key Takeaways:**
* **The Paradigm Shift:** Understanding why "Software 2.0" requires a different engineering mindset.
* **Optimization vs. Innovation:** How to position AI projects within a corporate strategy.
* **The Roadmap:** A high-level view of moving from zero to deploying Autonomous Agents.
