The Transformer architecture is the foundational neural network design behind Large Language Models (LLMs). It uses a mechanism called self-attention to understand the relationships and dependencies between words in a sequence, which is crucial for tasks like natural language understanding and generation. Unlike older models, Transformers process entire sequences at once rather than sequentially, allowing them to capture long-range context efficiently. The core components are encoders (which process input) and decoders (which generate output), though many LLMs use modified versions of these components.
