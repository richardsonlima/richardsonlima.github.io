# M.Sc. Research Journey – PG-EEC, ITA
### Master of Science in Electronics and Computer Engineering (Informatics)

This page tracks my academic progress, research milestones, and systematic mapping at the **Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica (ITA)**. My research focuses on the intersection of **Chaos Engineering** and **Dependability** in mission-critical financial ecosystems.

## 🎯 Research Core Objectives
The primary goal of this research is to create an **Assessment Model for Dependability Assurance** in critical systems. By utilizing **Chaos Engineering**-based experiments, we aim to evaluate system resilience and provide actionable architectural recommendations.

### Research Questions (RQs):

| ID | Question | Rationale |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| **RQ1** | Are there similar models to the one proposed? | Avoid plagiarism and redundancy. |
| **RQ2** | Which software types are currently developed for dependability validation? | Map open-source vs. commercial tool landscape. |
| **RQ3** | What are the main findings of linking Chaos Engineering with Dependability? | Support tactical and strategic resilience actions. |
| **RQ4** | Which geographical regions lead the scientific production in this field? | Map the global distribution of research. |
