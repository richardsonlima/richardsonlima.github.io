### "Echo of Tomorrow"

**Verse 1**

There’s a whisper in the tides,  
A herald of the days to come,  
Where every wave arrives carrying  
The hint of lives not yet begun.

**Chorus**

I feel it in the depths,  
Something just beyond the shore.  
Quiet as the horizon,  
Yet certain as the moon’s return once more.

**Verse 2**

At the edge of tomorrow,  
I trace the outline of the new.  
I step with steady courage,  
Knowing time is guiding me to you.

**Bridge**

Each step forward is a vow,  
A movement toward what has to be.  
Like oceans keeping rhythm,  
My path aligns with yours, patiently.

**Chorus**

I feel it in the depths,  
Something just beyond the shore.  
Quiet as the horizon,  
Yet certain as the moon’s return once more.

**Final**

When tomorrow stands before us  
And the waves set us in place,  
I will live each breath with clarity,  
For I foresaw this time and space.
