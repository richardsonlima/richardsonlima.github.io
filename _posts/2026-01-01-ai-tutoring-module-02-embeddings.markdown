Before a machine can think, it must first convert language into numbers. This module dives into the **embedding layer**, where discrete tokens are transformed into continuous vector representations. We explore how algorithms like **Byte Pair Encoding (BPE)** solve the vocabulary problem and how semantic meaning is encoded geometrically in high-dimensional spaces.

You will understand why "King - Man + Woman = Queen" works mathematically, and the limitations of static embeddings before they enter the Transformer blocks. This is the bridge between linguistics and linear algebra.
