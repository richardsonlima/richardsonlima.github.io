Not all Transformers are created equal. This module demystifies the architectural schism between **BERT (Encoders)** and **GPT (Decoders)**. The key difference lies in the **Attention Mask**: whether a token can see the future (bidirectional) or only the past (causal).

We analyze when to use each architecture for specific engineering tasks. While Decoders dominate generative AI, Encoders remain the kings of classification and semantic search. We also touch upon hybrid architectures like T5 and how to select the right engine for your Agent.
