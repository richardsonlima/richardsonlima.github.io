Research Context

Investigating the intersection of Chaos Engineering and Safety-Critical Systems within the aerospace domain. The focus lies on improving dependability in autonomous drone swarms through proactive fault injection.

Research Questions

RQ1: State of the Art

Are there Chaos Engineering models specifically adapted for Safety-Critical Systems?

Rationale: Identify gaps in current literature to avoid redundancy.
RQ2: Application Scope

What types of software in the aerospace industry (embedded, ground control, autonomous) are most suitable for Chaos experiments?

Rationale: Map the scenario where the contribution is most valuable.
RQ3: Dependability Impact

How can Chaos Engineering evidence support safety arguments in assurance cases?

Rationale: Connect technical findings with strategic certification needs.
