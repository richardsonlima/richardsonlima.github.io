Scientific note

There is consistent evidence that stable ambient sound can improve sustained focus by reducing external distractions, lowering attentional variability, and helping the brain maintain a steadier activation state during long cognitive tasks.

For study and deep work, non-lyrical, repetitive tracks with low sonic surprise tend to overload working memory less than complex vocal music. In meditation, the same rhythmic stability supports physiological regulation and attention to the present moment.

Practical outcome: fewer mental interruptions, more time in flow state, and more consistent performance throughout the session.
