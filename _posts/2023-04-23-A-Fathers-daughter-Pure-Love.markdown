A bond so steady, a love so true,  
between a father and his daughter too.  
Unconditional, patient, quiet, sure—  
a shelter of grace that will endure.

Through rises and falls, through thick and thin,  
his care does not fray, nor ever wear thin.  
A guiding hand, a gentle cue,  
a place to lean when days run blue.

Her bright regard, his lifelong pride,  
a thread that time can never divide.  
It will not dim, it will not fade,  
a promise kept, a covenant made.

Love that listens, love that stays,  
woven softly through the days.  
Two hearts learning how to be,  
strong together, wild and free.
